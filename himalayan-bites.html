The Story of Ghugute

In the hills of Uttarakhand, Ghugute is a special tradition celebrated around Makar Sankranti, as the sun begins its northward journey. The festival, also known as Ghughutia, welcomes Uttarayan with family, food and stories.

Folk stories tell of a clever crow who helped save a young prince. In gratitude, the queen prepared treats for the crows; the custom of sharing Ghugute on the first day of Uttarayan lives on in the celebration.

Traditionally, the dough is shaped into flowers, swords and damarus, then fried until golden. The treats are strung into garlands for children to wear and share. Himalayan Bites brings the warmth of this mountain custom to your table.
